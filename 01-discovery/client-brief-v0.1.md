## Why this brief is deliberately incomplete

A one-paragraph brief that names a solution category ("Hotel Management System") before establishing the business problem is a common real-world pattern — stakeholders often propose a solution before the underlying need is diagnosed. The discovery phase exists to separate the **requested solution** from the **actual problem**, consistent with the BA principle of distinguishing business needs from requested solutions.

See [discovery-questionnaire.md](discovery-questionnaire.md) for the structured interview built to close these gaps, and [interview-notes.md](interview-notes.md) for the record of what was actually confirmed.
