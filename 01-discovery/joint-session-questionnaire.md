## Proposed session structure

A short joint session (30-45 min) with all participants, to establish the shared problem and agree on scope — followed by separate targeted sessions: one with Group Finance, one with IT, and one with the two property GMs, to go through the deferred questions and review evidence in more depth.

## Joint session — essential questions only

A note before the questions: we're not asking what features or software you'd like. We're trying to understand how reporting and daily operations actually work today, and where the friction is. Please answer from your own day-to-day experience, not how things are "supposed to" work.

**Group Finance**
1. Walk us through the monthly process, start to end, for getting the consolidated revenue/occupancy report ready.
2. How long does that typically take, and what tends to cause the most delay or rework?
3. What decisions depend on this consolidated report?

**IT**
1. Does each of the five properties use the same operations/reservations system today, or different ones?
2. When corporate needs information from the properties, how does it currently get from each property's system into a usable report?
3. Without assuming it's necessarily a technology issue — what, if anything, currently makes it hard to get a single, comparable view across the five properties?

**Property General Managers** *(both participating GMs)*
1. How does your property record occupancy and revenue day to day, and how does the month-end report get put together?
2. Is there anything specific to your property that works differently from how you understand the other hotels operate?
3. What would reassure you that a new process or system wouldn't disrupt your daily front-desk and operational work?

**Everyone**
1. In one or two sentences: what's the single biggest frustration today with how information gets reported or shared?

## Deferred to targeted follow-up sessions

**Finance (follow-up)**
- How "room revenue" and "occupancy" are defined/calculated, and whether that definition is consistent across properties
- Other KPIs the group needs compared across properties
- Review of sample reports (see Evidence, below)

**IT (follow-up)**
- Whether any current systems are already connected to each other or to a central system
- Guest data, payment handling, or tourism-authority reporting considerations
- Existing contracts, licenses, or technical dependencies affecting timing

**Property GMs (follow-up)**
- Rough estimate of staff time spent on reporting each month
- Detailed walk-through of any manual workarounds in use

## Evidence requested

Only where approved internally for sharing, and with commercially sensitive figures or guest-identifiable information redacted or aggregated where it isn't needed to illustrate the issue:

- Sample monthly reports from 2-3 properties plus the final consolidated version (Finance)
- Any documented list of KPIs/metrics tracked per property (Finance / GMs)
- Current system names per property, where known (IT)
- Any existing data-flow diagram or integration map, if one exists (IT)
