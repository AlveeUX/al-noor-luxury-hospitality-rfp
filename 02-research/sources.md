- Regulatory requirements for emirates other than Dubai (Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, RAK, etc.)

## Research method

All research conducted via web search on 2026-10-04. Sources were prioritized by recency and specificity to the UAE/GCC hospitality market. Vendor-marketing sources were used only where no better alternative was found in this research pass, and are explicitly flagged as such rather than presented with the same confidence as independent reporting.
