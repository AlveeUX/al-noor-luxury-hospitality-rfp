# Vendor Evaluation Matrix — Group KPI Governance & Consolidation Capability

**Status: Draft — BA-proposed weighting, pending Procurement (Khalid) and Executive sign-off.** No evaluation committee has been formally confirmed (discovery questionnaire Q4.2 was never answered) — until it is, treat these weights as a starting proposal, not an approved scoring model.

## Scoring model

| Criterion | Weight | What it measures | Scored from |
|---|---|---|---|
| Functional compliance | 30% | Coverage of FR-01–FR-12 per the compliance matrix | [`vendor-response-template.md`](vendor-response-template.md) §3 |
| Non-functional & compliance fit | 20% | PDPL compliance, auditability, usability, role-based access | §3, §5 |
| Implementation approach & risk | 15% | Fit with phased, pilot-first rollout; migration/adoption risk | §4 |
| Vendor experience & references | 10% | Relevant domain experience, reference quality | §1, §8 |
| Commercial terms | 15% | Total cost of ownership, pricing transparency | §7 |
| Support model | 10% | SLA adequacy, escalation clarity | §6 |

Each criterion scored 1–5 per vendor; weighted score = Σ(criterion score × weight).
