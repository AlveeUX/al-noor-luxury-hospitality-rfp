# Delivery Phasing — Group KPI Governance & Consolidation Capability

**Status: Draft.** Phases are sequential and gated — a later phase should not start before its gating criteria are met, even if resourcing is available, because doing so has already been identified as a risk (T1, T6 in [`../05-technology/risks-and-dependencies.md`](../05-technology/risks-and-dependencies.md)).

| Phase | Objective | Key activities | Exit / gating criteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0 — Governance Foundation** | Establish the governance layer before anything is built or bought | Draft occupancy KPI definition; run property-GM consultation; secure Executive approval of Finance ownership; draft escalation policy | US-01 (occupancy), US-02, US-03, and US-07 all confirmed/approved |
| **1 — Pilot** | Prove the approach on the single confirmed KPI-level finding (occupancy) before expanding | Build-vs-buy decision; stand up pilot (validate → consolidate → review → report) for occupancy only, one or two properties | Pilot runs one full monthly cycle without regressing the current process; Finance confirms the validate-first step actually reduces round-trips |
| **2 — Process Rollout** | Extend the validated approach to all five properties for occupancy | Full rollout of Phase 1's approach; case logging and categorization live (US-06) | All five properties submitting through the validated process for at least one cycle |
| **3 — KPI Expansion** | Add revenue, ADR, RevPAR **only as each is independently confirmed affected** | Validate each candidate KPI's actual definitional exposure before adding it to the dictionary | Each KPI has its own confirmed finding, not an assumption carried from occupancy |
| **4 — Regulatory Reporting Integration** | Close the regulatory tracked dependency | Complete property-by-property inventory (US-09); design integration only after | US-09 fully resolved — **this phase cannot start earlier, regardless of other phases' progress** |
| **5 — Property D Onboarding** | Bring Property D to parity with Properties A–C | Confirm PMS/flow (US-10); apply Phases 0–3's governance and process | US-10 fully resolved |
| **6 — Hypercare & Review** | Confirm the original objectives were actually met | Monitor adoption; review against candidate success criteria ([`../03-requirements/business-requirements-document.md`](../03-requirements/business-requirements-document.md) §8) | Formal review session held with Khalid, Nadia, and Omar |

## Notes on sequencing

- Phases 4 and 5 can run in parallel with Phases 1–3 once their own gating dependencies resolve — they are not strictly serial with the rest, only internally gated on their own dependency.
- No phase has a committed date. Per the project's standing position on undisclosed timeline/budget, this phasing describes **order**, not a calendar.
