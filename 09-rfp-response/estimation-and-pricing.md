## Basis of estimate

Effort is broken down by phase and role, converted to days at an illustrative blended day rate per role. This covers Phases 0–7 (occupancy only, four confirmed properties) from [Timeline](timeline.md) — it does not include revenue/ADR/RevPAR expansion, regulatory reporting integration, or the fifth property's onboarding, consistent with those being out of scope for this response (see Scope of Work).

## What would change this number

- **Build vs. buy.** This is priced as a build engagement. A buy decision replaces most of Development (Phase 3) and part of UI (Phase 2) with configuration and integration effort instead — very likely a lower total, but not estimated here since the decision hasn't been made.
- **KPI expansion, regulatory integration, Property D.** Each would be priced as a separate addition once its dependency resolves — bundling a number into this estimate now would mean pricing scope that isn't confirmed (same discipline the RFP itself applies to its own estimation model).
- **Phase 0 delays.** If governance alignment takes longer than two weeks, Phases 1 onward shift — this proposal doesn't assume a compressed Phase 0 to make the total look smaller.
