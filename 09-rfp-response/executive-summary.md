# Executive Summary — RFP Response

**Practice-exercise note:** This folder is a self-directed portfolio exercise — practicing the *agency side* of an RFP (responding to one), as a counterpart to the RFP itself in `06-rfp-and-commercial/`. It is written in the voice of a generic digital product agency, not any real company, and no claims in this folder (team composition, rates) should be read as real. See the repository's own [README.md](../README.md) for the same disclosure about Al Noor.

---

In response to **Al Noor Luxury Hospitality Group's** Request for Proposal (AL-NOOR-PROC-2026-01), we propose a phased, governance-first approach to resolving the group's KPI definition and consolidation reporting gap.

**The problem, in one line:** Al Noor's five properties don't disagree on data — they disagree on *definitions*. No KPI dictionary exists, consolidation runs through a manual Excel layer outside any property's PMS, and there's no formal process for resolving issues when the reporting deadline arrives. The RFP correctly does not ask for a new Hotel Management System, and we agree with that framing — replacing a PMS would not touch the actual problem.

**What we propose:** a lightweight **Group KPI Governance & Consolidation Workspace** — a validate-before-consolidate workflow, a versioned KPI dictionary, a clarification-case log, a severity-based escalation path, and a documented property-variance mechanism — delivered against a single confirmed KPI (occupancy) first, piloted at one or two properties, and only then rolled out group-wide. We are not proposing to replace any property's PMS, and our approach explicitly protects each property's legitimate operating differences rather than standardizing them away.

**Why us:** we specialize in exactly this kind of problem — turning an ambiguous, partially-scoped brief into a buildable, estimable product, in close collaboration with the client's own stakeholders rather than assuming we already know the answer. Our process is discovery-led: before UI work starts, we validate the KPI dictionary and governance model with Finance, IT, and Property GMs directly, so the tool reflects an agreed process rather than encoding Al Noor's current ambiguity faster.

**What this response contains:** our understanding of the problem in our own words, the proposed solution and approach, scope of work, delivery phases, a timeline, the team we'd staff, an effort and pricing estimate, our assumptions/risks/dependencies, and the next step to move this forward.

**One open item we'd want resolved before kickoff:** the RFP itself flags that Al Noor hasn't yet decided between buying a configurable platform and building bespoke (§0 of the RFP). This response is scoped for a **build** engagement — if Al Noor confirms a buy decision instead, our role shifts from building to configuring and integrating a selected platform, and this estimate would need to be revised accordingly.
