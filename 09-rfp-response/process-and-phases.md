# Process and Phases

Our standard delivery process — discovery, UX, UI, development, testing, launch — applied to this engagement's governance-first, pilot-first constraint. We don't run a conventional single pass through these stages; governance validation and a one-property pilot gate the move from design into full build, and full rollout is gated again behind a successful pilot cycle.

## Phase 0 — Discovery & Governance Alignment

Joint sessions with Finance, IT, and Property GMs to validate the occupancy KPI definition, confirm the governance process (who proposes, who's consulted, who approves), and draft the escalation policy. No screens are designed in this phase. This phase also surfaces whether the build-vs-buy decision and Executive sign-off on Finance's ownership role are resolved enough to proceed.

**Exit criteria:** occupancy definition agreed; governance process and escalation policy documented; build-vs-buy decision confirmed.

## Phase 1 — UX Design

Workflow design for the five core flows (propose/approve a definition, validate and consolidate, log a case, escalate an issue, submit a variance), working from the governance process agreed in Phase 0, not before it. Information architecture and wireframe-level screen flows, reviewed with Finance and at least one Property GM before moving to UI.

**Exit criteria:** core flows and IA signed off by Finance.

## Phase 2 — UI Design

Visual design applied to the signed-off UX flows, including a localization/RTL and accessibility pass (see Scope of Work) and Al Noor's brand application if provided.

**Exit criteria:** UI designs approved for build.

## Phase 3 — Development

Build against the approved designs, scoped to occupancy only and the four confirmed properties. Internal sprints with regular client demos rather than a single reveal at the end — Al Noor should be able to flag a misunderstanding early, not after four weeks of silent build time.

**Exit criteria:** feature-complete build ready for QA.

## Phase 4 — QA & Testing

Functional testing against the acceptance criteria agreed in Phase 0, plus a focused usability check with Finance (the primary daily user) and at least one Property GM.

**Exit criteria:** no open critical/high-severity defects; usability check passed.

## Phase 5 — Pilot Launch

One full monthly cycle, at one or two properties, running in parallel with Al Noor's existing process (not replacing it yet) — this is a deliberate safeguard, not a scheduling convenience. We're validating that the validate-first step actually reduces round-trips before asking all five properties to depend on it.

**Exit criteria:** pilot cycle completed without regressing the current process; Finance confirms the validate-first step reduces round-trips.

## Phase 6 — Full Rollout

Extend to all four confirmed properties (the fifth joins once its system and reporting flow are confirmed, as a separately scoped addition).

**Exit criteria:** all four properties submitting through the validated process for at least one full cycle.

## Phase 7 — Hypercare & Review

A defined support window post-rollout, plus a formal review session against the objectives in the RFP before we'd call this engagement's core scope complete.

**Exit criteria:** formal review session held; agreed objectives assessed against actual results.

---

**Deliberately out of this phasing:** revenue/ADR/RevPAR dictionary expansion, regulatory reporting integration, and the fifth property's onboarding. Each is a real, anticipated piece of future work — but pricing or scheduling it now would mean estimating against scope that isn't confirmed yet. We'd scope each as an addition once its own dependency resolves (see Assumptions, Risks & Dependencies).
