# Team

Roles are described generically (no individual names), since staffing is confirmed at kickoff based on actual availability. Allocation shows involvement by phase, not headcount — several roles are part-time across multiple concurrent engagements.

| Role | Responsibility on this engagement | Primarily active in |
|---|---|---|
| Business Analyst / Project Lead | Owns discovery, requirements validation, scope, and acts as the single point of contact for Al Noor throughout | All phases |
| UX Designer | Workflow design, information architecture, wireframes; runs the Phase 1 review sessions with Finance and Property GMs | Phase 1, consulted through 2–4 |
| UI Designer | Visual design, including the localization/RTL and accessibility pass | Phase 2, consulted through 3–4 |
| Backend Developer | Data model, API, validation logic, audit/change-history implementation | Phase 3–4 |
| Frontend Developer | Workspace build against approved UI designs | Phase 3–4 |
| QA Engineer | Test planning, functional and usability testing | Phase 4, light involvement through 5–6 |
| Delivery / Account Manager | Commercial point of contact, status reporting, escalation path on our side if something in the engagement itself needs resolving | All phases, lighter touch during 3–4 |

## How this maps to collaboration across Sales, Design, and Development

The Business Analyst / Project Lead is the handoff point in both directions: taking Al Noor's requirements and governance decisions into a scope Design and Development can act on, and taking Design/Development's constraints or questions back to Al Noor rather than letting them sit unresolved inside the team. Design and Development are looped into Phase 0 discovery readouts even before their own phases start, so neither is seeing the problem for the first time when their phase begins.

## What this team does not include

No dedicated DevOps/infrastructure role is scoped — hosting and deployment approach depends on the build-vs-buy decision and Al Noor's own IT environment, and would be scoped once that's confirmed. No dedicated localization/translation resource is included beyond the UI Designer's RTL pass — if Arabic content translation (not just layout) is required, that's a separate, as-yet-unconfirmed scope item (see Assumptions, Risks & Dependencies).
