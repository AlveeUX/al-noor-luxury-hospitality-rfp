# Timeline

**Starting point:** this timeline begins from a confirmed kickoff — build decided (not buy), SOW signed, and Al Noor's stakeholders (Finance, IT, at least one Property GM) available for the sessions each phase needs. It covers Phases 0–7 from [Process and Phases](process-and-phases.md): occupancy only, the four confirmed properties. It does **not** include revenue/ADR/RevPAR expansion, regulatory reporting integration, or the fifth property — those are scoped once their own dependencies resolve, not estimated here.

| Phase | Duration | Cumulative |
|---|---|---|
| 0 — Discovery & Governance Alignment | 2 weeks | Week 2 |
| 1 — UX Design | 3 weeks | Week 5 |
| 2 — UI Design | 2 weeks | Week 7 |
| 3 — Development | 6 weeks | Week 13 |
| 4 — QA & Testing | 2 weeks | Week 15 |
| 5 — Pilot Launch (one full monthly cycle) | 4 weeks | Week 19 |
| 6 — Full Rollout | 3 weeks | Week 22 |
| 7 — Hypercare & Review | 4 weeks | Week 26 |

**Indicative total: ~26 weeks (roughly 6 months)** from confirmed kickoff to formal review session.

## Why this is a range, not a commitment

- **Phase 0's duration depends on stakeholder availability**, not build effort — if Executive sign-off on Finance's ownership role or property-GM consultation sessions take longer to schedule than two weeks, this phase (and everything after it) shifts by the same amount. This is the single biggest timeline risk in the whole engagement.
- **Phase 5 is a full monthly cycle by design** — it cannot be compressed without undermining the pilot's purpose, which is to prove the validate-first step actually works before every property depends on it.
- **This assumes a build engagement.** If Al Noor decides to buy a configurable platform instead, Phases 1–3 are replaced by a configuration and integration effort, which would very likely shorten this timeline — we'd re-scope it once that decision is made.

## Not included in this timeline

No go-live date is fixed here, because none has been set by Al Noor (the RFP says so explicitly, and we're not going to invent one). This table shows *duration from kickoff*, not calendar dates — once a kickoff date is confirmed, we'd convert this into a dated plan within the same week.
